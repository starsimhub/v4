Library modules

Starsim also comes with a library of example modules, written as code, which are illustrative rather than validated: their parameter values are indicative, and some are placeholders. Use them as starting points to copy and adapt. They are imported with import starsim.library as ssl:

  • Diseases (ssl.diseases): HIV with CD4 counts (with ssl.diseases.ART for treatment), cholera with an environmental reservoir, Ebola with transmission from unburied bodies, measles as an SEIR, and a non-communicable disease.
  • Networks (ssl.networks): households from survey data, people who move in space and connect to those nearby (DiskNet), and theoretical networks such as Erdős–Rényi.
  • Maternal, newborn, and child health (ssl.mnch): fetal health, neonatal sepsis, and congenital disease.

Library modules in the user guide describes them, and how to use them.

Scripts in the repository

A few longer examples are kept as scripts in the docs/examples folder of the repository, to read and adapt:

  • An embedding network (networks/embedding.py): pairs people by their closeness in a continuous space of traits.
  • One model in several languages (translations/): the same simple SIS model written in plain Python, Numba, JAX, Julia, and Rust, next to the Starsim version, for comparing their speed.
  • LASER (laser/): a LASER example and its Starsim equivalents, with scripts to time them.
