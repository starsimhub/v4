solvers.deliveries

solvers.deliveries

Not for the user: deliveries in a compartmental run

ss.Event (and its subclass ss.Delivery) is the one class for events and deliveries, and its methods are how it runs as agents. In a compartmental run, a delivery also makes a CompartmentalDelivery (delivery.in_compartments), which holds what only that needs: a selection is the fraction of the people in each state and group, so who is reached, served, moved, cured, or tested is a fraction of each, and moving them moves fractions of the engine’s compartments between groups, carrying the events of the step along. The schedule, coverage, capacity, conditions, products, and results stay the delivery’s.

Used with: compartments; declared modules

carry_events
solvers.deliveries.CompartmentalDelivery.carry_events(
    frac,
    before,
    mover=None,
    target=None,
    dst=None,
)

Carry the events of this step (see ss.Event.fire()) along a move the delivery has just made, between groups (to target) or into a state (dst), so that the steps that read them later in the step find their people where they now are. Of the people of an event, those moved are: all the movers, if the movers are within the event (e.g. a step it triggered, mover); none, if they are disjoint from it; and otherwise the same fraction as of everyone in their state and group.

cure_joint
solvers.deliveries.CompartmentalDelivery.cure_joint(chosen, diseases)

The treatment of several diseases: the people chosen in each compartment and group are moved as a population of their own (with force()), so the fraction forced is chosen × probability in each, and the rest of them have failed. Diseases tracked jointly (see ss.solvers.JointStates) pass the same people on, by way of the joint array, so each sees those the others treated. Its outcomes are events as fractions of this disease’s states and groups; someone cured of one disease and failed for another counts in both, and anyone in both outcomes for two diseases counts twice (clipped at all)

In a compartmental run: carry the events of this step along a move of the people of one of them (by a transition that event triggered: see trigger_groups()), from the people it took out of each state and group to those it put in, so a step that reads them later in the step finds them where they are now.
