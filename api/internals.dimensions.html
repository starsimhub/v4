internals.dimensions

internals.dimensions

Dimensions: how a model is divided into groups by the properties of people (age, sex, place, …)

A stratification is declared once and read by every module and solver, as levels held by each agent or as groups of compartments.

Used with: agents and compartments; declared and coded modules

Classes

Name Description
Groups The groups of a sim: its stratifications (ss.dimensions.Strat), and how their levels combine into groups
Strat One stratification: a property of people, and the levels it is split into
Transfer Movement between the levels of a stratification, without a change of disease state: see ss.transfer(), which makes it

Groups

internals.dimensions.Groups(sim)

The groups of a sim: its stratifications (ss.dimensions.Strat), and how their levels combine into groups

In a compartmental run, each group is a row of every compartment; as agents, each agent is in one group, which is worked out from their properties when needed (see group_raw()). Made when the sim is initialized, as sim.groups.

group_raw
internals.dimensions.Groups.group_raw()

In an agent-based run: the group of every agent, as an array indexed by UID

It is kept until groups_changed() says it has changed, and then only the groups of the agents named are worked out again. A dimension binned from a property that changes on its own, such as age, is checked again on every step.

init_ragged
internals.dimensions.Groups.init_ragged()

For a dimension with where= (e.g. age groups for women only): which groups have a level of it

The groups stay a grid, with a level of every dimension. People in a group outside a dimension’s where= have no level of it, so they are all counted at its first level (the “fold” of their group), as people in a state that a dimension does not apply to are; the groups at its other levels stay empty.

Strat

internals.dimensions.Strat(
    name,
    levels,
    split=None,
    adjust=None,
    kind=None,
    states=None,
    where=None,
    mobility=None,
)

One stratification: a property of people, and the levels it is split into

Parameters

Name Type Description Default
name str the dimension, e.g. ‘age’ required
levels list / dict bin edges (for a continuous property such as age) or labels; or a dict of the share of the population in each, e.g. {0: 0.3, 15: 0.6, 65: 0.1} required
split list the share of the population in each level (if not given: from an ss.geo.geography’s populations; for age groups, the world’s ages; for a boolean dimension, everyone at the first level; otherwise equal) None
adjust dict multipliers on named transitions, one per level: on its rate (a duration is divided), or on its probability for a branch chosen with p= None
kind str ‘age’, ‘space’, or ‘plain’; if not given, ‘space’ for an ss.geo.geography or with mobility=, ‘age’ for a dimension called age, and otherwise ‘plain’ (a strain dimension, kind ‘strain’, is made by ss.Strains: see strains) None
states list the names of the states it applies to (default: every state that is people) None
where dict the groups it applies to, as a dimension and a level (or list of levels), e.g. dict(sex='female'); people in other groups have no level of it (default: every group) None
mobility gravity / radiation / Mobility / array for a spatial stratification, the movement between its levels None

Attributes

Name Description
attr The name of the property of people that holds each agent’s level; a dimension with labels that is named like something people already have (age) is kept apart from it
mids The middle of each bin, for a binned property

same_as Whether another stratification is this one given again: the same levels, kind, split, adjustments, and states
shares The share of each group in its level of this dimension (or in levels, if given): from the split, or, for a split given the levels of other dimensions, from the row of that split for the group

init_carriage
internals.dimensions.Strat.init_carriage(strains)

For a strain dimension whose levels are sets of strains carried at once (‘A+B’, from ss.Strains(max_strains=)): which strains each level carries, and where carrying one more leads

The first levels are the strains alone. Someone who carries several strains passes on one of them (see ss.Strains(transmit=)), and someone infected with a strain they do not carry moves to the level that adds it (once full, the level that replaces the oldest: see ss.Strains._adds()).

Transfer

internals.dimensions.Transfer(
    rate=None,
    dur=None,
    p=None,
    states=None,
    name=None,
    label=None,
    increment=None,

Movement between the levels of a stratification, without a change of disease state: see ss.transfer(), which makes it

It exists so that movement such as aging or moving between risk groups is declared beside the disease, not as one transition per state.

Functions

Name Description
transfer Movement between the levels of a stratification, without a change of disease state

transfer

internals.dimensions.transfer(
    rate=None,
    dur=None,
    p=None,
    states=None,
    name=None,
    label=None,
    increment=None,
    **kwargs,
)

Movement between the levels of a stratification, without a change of disease state

People move all the time, at a rate or after a duration, and the transfer is part of the model: pass it to ss.Disease() or stratify(). A move delivered to some people, at some times, is a delivery: ss.Delivery(move=dict(vaccinated=True), coverage=0.7, at=[2021]).

Parameters

Name Type Description Default
rate any the per-person hazard None
dur any the time before moving (instead of rate): a scalar is the mean of an exponential, and a distribution is drawn for each person when they reach a level it moves them from None
p list with several levels to move to (method=('none', ['pill', 'iud'])), the probability of each, decided as people move; one may be None, for the rest None
states list the disease states it applies to (default: every state) None
name str the name; if not given, taken from the keyword or variable it is assigned to None
increment str / dict instead of a level, a counter that goes up by one (its name), or by an amount (dict(schooling=1), dict(immunity=-1)) None
kwargs dict the dimension, and a pair of its levels, from and to (e.g. risk=('low', 'high')), or the level people move to, from every other level (e.g. vaccinated=True) {}

Examples

ss.transfer(risk=('low', 'high'), rate=ss.peryear(0.1))
ss.transfer(vaccinated=True, rate=ss.peryear(0.2))
ss.transfer(increment=dict(immunity=-1), rate=ss.peryear(0.5)) # A counter, down a level at a time
ss.transfer(method=('none', ['pill', 'iud']), p=[0.6, 0.4], dur=ss.lognorm_ex(ss.years(2), ss.years(1))) # To one of two levels, after a time drawn for each person
