pregnancy

pregnancy

Pregnancy, declared: its states and transitions are the same objects as a disease’s, so it runs as agents or as compartments, and other modules can use its events (preg.conception, preg.delivery) and states (preg.P)

Lactation

pregnancy.Lactation(pars=None, pregnancy=None, **kwargs)

Lactation, as declared states: N (not breastfeeding) and L (breastfeeding)

A sibling of ss.Pregnancy (or of a subclass of it, by any name), since lactation overlaps the next pregnancy. A live birth starts it (with p_breastfeed), or restarts it for a woman breastfeeding already; it ends after dur_breastfeed, or at once if a child of hers dies before the age of 1. A BreastfeedingNet pairs each newborn with a mother who is in L and ends the pair when she leaves it. Added by ss.Pregnancy when the sim has a BreastfeedingNet (lactation='auto'). Agents only, since it is triggered by events of each mother and her children.

Parameters

dur_breastfeed dur / Dist duration of breastfeeding required
pregnancy str / Pregnancy the pregnancy module whose events drive it, or its name (default: the sim’s only ss.Pregnancy, of any subclass) None

Examples

preg, lact = ss.Pregnancy(fertility_rate=ss.freqperyear(80)), ss.Lactation()
lam = ss.modify(preg.conception, multiply=0.1, where=lact.L & (preg.since_birth < ss.months(6)))

Pregnancy

pregnancy.Pregnancy(
    pars=None,
    fetus='auto',
    lactation='auto',
    metadata=None,
    **kwargs,
)

Pregnancy, as declared states and transitions

Everyone is in NP (not pregnant: men and children too) or P (pregnant). Gestational age is P.time. Conception is one transition, so contraception, HIV, or LAM are modifiers of it (ss.modify(preg.conception, multiply=...)). A pregnancy ends in an abortion (at once, with p_abortion), a miscarriage or stillbirth (a hazard before or after loss_threshold), a maternal death (antepartum), or a delivery at the end of a dwell of dur_pregnancy. Each delivery gives embryos_per_pregnancy children (birth): as agents, new agents whose parent is the mother, placed in each declared module’s state given by its own ss.birth(per=preg.delivery) (or else its first state). Children die before the age of 1 at infant_rate (infant_mortality; 0 by default). It runs as agents under solver='auto'; a compartmental solver named explicitly runs a small approximate form (see compartmental_form()).

For other modules, preg.delivery happens to the mother, and to her fetuses (if they are agents: see _init_fetus()) before they are born, so a death of a fetus on=preg.delivery is a stillbirth; preg.birth happens to the newborns, so a neonatal death is on=preg.birth.

Its parameters are listed in __init__(); code reacts to its events with add_callback(), e.g. preg.add_callback(preg.birth, fn). Its states and results are P (pregnant), ti_P, the scheduled delivery ti_exit_P (set_delivery() reschedules it), and the flows conception, birth, miscarriage, stillbirth, and abortion.

burnin_plan
pregnancy.Pregnancy.burnin_plan(engine)

burnin=True (the default): women pregnant at the start. Each woman who could conceive has the chance of having conceived in the mean duration of a pregnancy before the start, at her conception hazard then (1 - exp(-h*dur)), at a step uniform over it, and is run forward by the pregnancy’s own transitions (abortion, losses, and delivery, with no births), so the start has the gestational ages and the first steps their deliveries. In the compartmental form, the same share of each group, less the abortions and the losses on the way, is spread evenly over gestation (see ss.solvers.Compartmental.backfill_burnin()).

compartmental_form
pregnancy.Pregnancy.compartmental_form(sim)

The small compartmental form, used only when a compartmental solver is named (solver='auto' runs a pregnancy as agents): the states NP and P, conception, abortion, maternal deaths, and the delivery at the end of dur_pregnancy (a fixed delay, or Erlang stages for a distribution), with n children per delivery (ss.birth(per=preg.delivery)). The losses do not read the gestational age (P.time): the loss rate is split between miscarriage and stillbirth by the share of the mean gestation below loss_threshold. It has no fetuses, no links between mothers and children, no lactation, no preterm risk, and no deaths at delivery (p_maternal_death). Returns the note that says so

compute_asfr
pregnancy.Pregnancy.compute_asfr()

The age-specific fertility rates of the step (per 1000 women: deliveries by the mother’s age, over the women of that age), and the TFR from them. As compartments, by the age of each group, so it needs an age stratification (otherwise NaN)

conception_rate
pregnancy.Pregnancy.conception_rate(sim)

The hazard of conception, per year, for each agent (by UID) or each group: the fertility rate (in the year a pregnancy starting now would end, if given by year), rescaled from all fecund women to those not pregnant; 0 for anyone not fecund

An override can instead take (sim, uids) and return a rate for each of those agents (as agents only), e.g. ss.peryear(table[self.method[uids]]), rather than one for every slot of the raw arrays.

preterm_risk
pregnancy.Pregnancy.preterm_risk(uids)

At conception: each woman’s relative risk of preterm birth (rel_ptb), her baseline (rr_ptb) times the risk at her age (rr_ptb_age), multiplies her probability of a preterm duration (below preterm_threshold), capped at 1; the durations within the preterm and the term ones keep their shares. For dur_pregnancy as an ss.choice (the default), each duration is drawn so (one uniform number, by inverse transform), kept in weeks (dur_gestation, the gestational age at birth), and the delivery scheduled by it, as the engine schedules a dwell time (agents only)

run_callbacks
pregnancy.Pregnancy.run_callbacks()

The callbacks (see add_callback()) see the step’s new pregnancies once their fetuses and preterm risk are set, so a callback’s set_delivery() is kept

set_delivery
pregnancy.Pregnancy.set_delivery(uids, ti, weeks=None)

Reschedule the deliveries of some pregnant women to a step (its index, as ti_exit_P reads; each scheduled exit of P is the delivery: losses are rates, which compete with it), with the gestational age they will have then (dur_gestation): weeks if given, otherwise the weeks from conception to that step

step
pregnancy.Pregnancy.step()

The pregnancy’s transitions, at the start of the step, before the diseases’

step_die
pregnancy.Pregnancy.step_die(uids)

As agents: take the dead out of the states. The death of the last fetus of a pregnancy is a miscarriage or a stillbirth for the mother, by gestational age; a mother who dies while pregnant loses her fetuses, counted the same way; and a death before the age of 1 is an infant death for the mother.
